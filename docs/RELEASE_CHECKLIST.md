# Mandatory release gates
- [ ] Licensed clinician reviews and signs each exercise's technique, dosage, indications, exclusion criteria, progression and regression rules.
- [ ] Clinician reviews all onboarding red flag questions, triage, fall risk and emergency copy.
- [ ] Verify professional video licenses or film our own correctly demonstrated movement media; YouTube professional status is not commercial re-use permission.
- [ ] Regulatory counsel assesses intended use and FDA general-wellness/medical-device boundaries and marketing claims.
- [ ] Privacy counsel assesses FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, applicable state health-data law, consent, deletion, security, age rules and processor agreements.
- [ ] Conduct accessibility testing with adults 50–70, including readable UI and audio/video instruction.
- [ ] Adversarial integration tests (empty answers, unclear falls, novel symptoms, after-check-in restrictions), audit logs, and security tests.
- [ ] Clinical supervision + informed consent + escalation procedures for any real-user feasibility pilot; document adverse events and outcomes.
- [ ] Production clinical approvals granted per exercise and pathway: until then delivery remains disabled.
