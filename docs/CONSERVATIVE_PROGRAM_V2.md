# Conservative Mobility 60: Program Design v2 (NOT a clinical prescription)

## What changed
- Source-of-truth remains the 15 uploaded professional-video transcripts and original source mapping. Original exercise records, clinical audit, intake questionnaire, pathways and API remain included.
- All 61 candidates remain archived and mapped to sources; **13 low-complexity introductory candidates** are available **only for review and UI mockups**, and none is cleared for live assignment.
- No one is promised a pain cure, freedom from injury, or a guaranteed 60-day transformation. No activity is universally risk-free.
- No unsupervised balance tasks, eyes-closed drills, floor transfers, deep kneeling, weighted squats, stair/step-up tests, unassessed shoulder or back pain protocols, post-surgical rehab, or independent directional-preference tests.

## 60-day design (not exercise orders)
- Days 1–3: questionnaire, safety screening, baseline subjective function and goals; NO automatic movement tests.
- Days 4–14: short, comfortable introductory movements on eligible days, review-only. One set of ~5 controlled repetitions is a **design proposal**, not clinically authorized dosage; easy seated marching may be proposed in 20–30-second increments.
- Days 15–30: repeat the tolerated foundation; after reviewer approval consider one additional set, maximum two strength sets per exercise in this starter concept. Never automatically increase because of the calendar.
- Days 31–45: maintain or gently progress only one approved parameter at a time after repeated comfortable sessions; otherwise hold or regress.
- Days 46–60: rehearse relevant everyday abilities and recheck self-reported activity goals; do not promise improvement.
- Suggested weekly layout for review: 2 nonconsecutive strength days, 2 gentle movement/walking days, 2 recovery/education/check-in days, 1 review day. No requirement to exercise vigorously daily.
- Default session UX target 5–12 minutes **subject to tolerance and approval**; time is not a guarantee of safety.

## Starter candidates (no prescription or automatic approval)
- Seated march, knee extension, calf/toe raises.
- Supported chair rise or mini squat (choose one, not both by default); supported hip hinge.
- Wall push-up; supported hip extension; relaxed walking as individually tolerated.
- Chin retraction, gentle scapular W; supported sit-back; pelvic-tilt heel slide.
- These are examples for independently mobile adults without identified restrictions, not blanket clearance.

## Adaptation and hard stops
- Complete all required screening; uncertainty or missing answers => no plan.
- New chest pain, fainting, significant breathlessness, concerning neurological symptoms => stop, appropriate urgent care advice.
- Fall/near fall, dizziness, recent surgery, acute injury, clinician restriction, marked symptom worsening => no automatic session; medical assessment/review.
- Pain worsening or inability to perform a movement comfortably => withhold progression and review/regress only after approved rules.
- Progression, regression and modifications MUST be signed off for specific movements and user groups. Not a chat-generated diagnosis.

## Source integrity
- The source videos describe larger amounts for some moves (e.g. 2–3 sets of 10, balance exercises); proposed lower research doses are our **product-design adjustment** and are **not directly validated in the transcripts**.
- Some videos overstate efficacy and universal safety; claims about certain causes of pain, guaranteed fixes, or rapid recovery are excluded.
- Changing numbers of sets alone does not eliminate risk. Do not release without individual exercise eligibility and adverse-event escalation review.
