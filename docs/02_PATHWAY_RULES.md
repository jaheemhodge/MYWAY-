# Four draft pathways — no automatic clinical authorization

All four are **design candidates**. Every pathway is initially disabled for real customers; review its exercises, dosage and triggers before enablement. No unverified AI exercise selection.

## walk_capacity
- Goal: Build comfortable walking tolerance
- Baseline: Self-reported walking duration and activity interference
- Entry: Independently mobile, no red flags, no relevant restrictions or unassessed falls
- Starting option: Brief comfortable walking; optional low-tier chair strength after approval
- Progression: After repeated tolerable sessions and stable baseline, clinician-approved small change to ONE parameter
- Regression: Shorter walking duration, more recovery, supported alternatives
- Stop/escalate: New chest symptoms, dizziness, worsening weakness, serious pain or new falls; referral

## chair_rise
- Goal: Make standing from chairs easier
- Baseline: Self-reported chair-rise difficulty; standardized test only after clinician approval
- Entry: Safe chair environment and no instability or recent surgery
- Starting option: Arm-assisted sit-to-stand and/or seated leg exercise if approved
- Progression: Progress aid, repetitions, or seat height one at a time per reviewed criteria
- Regression: Higher chair, arm support, fewer repetitions
- Stop/escalate: New falls/near falls, marked pain increase, buckling or concerning symptoms

## workday_capacity
- Goal: Make workday standing and moving more manageable
- Baseline: Functional interference at work; tolerable duration on feet
- Entry: Screen exertional symptoms, occupational demands, pain causes and environment
- Starting option: Gentle movement breaks, low-tier strength and comfortable activity
- Progression: Increase volume on tolerated days only, not fixed 10–20% weekly
- Regression: Shorter bouts, more breaks, fewer loaded movements
- Stop/escalate: Exertional chest symptoms, faintness, marked swelling or worsening symptoms

## morning_mobility
- Goal: Move more comfortably on waking
- Baseline: Minutes until usual comfortable movement and difficulty with first daily activity
- Entry: Exclude inflammatory, rapidly worsening or otherwise concerning presentations from automated pathway
- Starting option: Low-amplitude comfortable seated movements, optional short walk
- Progression: Expand comfortable range or time only as suitable
- Regression: Less range, shorter duration, sitting alternatives
- Stop/escalate: New neurologic symptoms, severe night/rest pain or sustained worsening stiffness

## Common review rules
- A 60-day program is a **calendar with recovery days**, not 60 consecutive workouts or a promise of a result by day 60.
- First 1–2 weeks establish tolerability; thereafter changes require repeat tolerability checks, not time-based advancement alone.
- Only one parameter changes per progression attempt, contingent on clinician-set thresholds.
- Feedback fields: ability to complete activity, symptoms before/after and next day, perceived effort, falls/near-falls, new symptoms, fatigue and confidence.
- If user misses sessions, do not compensate with a larger workload.
- No automated specific pain treatment, balance/fall prevention, rehab after surgery or floor-transfer exercises in MVP.
- Reassess functional goals at baseline, day 14, day 30 and day 60; never promise pain elimination.
